FleetDesk Service

File Audit & Cleanup

We review existing records, identify gaps and turn the mess into an organized system.

What you are actually paying FleetDesk to do

Step 1 — inventory

We review the administrative records you provide and create an inventory of what is present.

Step 2 — organize

Existing documents are renamed/filed into a consistent structure so each driver, vehicle or workflow can be reviewed.

Step 3 — gap report

You receive an outstanding-items report identifying records that appear missing, expired or still need follow-up based on the agreed administrative checklist.

Step 4 — remediation support

We follow up for replacement records and update the system as corrected information arrives.

Important boundary

This is an administrative records review, not a legal opinion, agency audit or guarantee of regulatory compliance.
