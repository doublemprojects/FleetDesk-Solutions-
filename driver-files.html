FleetDesk Service

Driver Files & Onboarding

We keep driver paperwork organized from hire through ongoing file maintenance.

What you are actually paying FleetDesk to do

What this service is

FleetDesk creates and maintains an organized digital administrative file for each driver so required paperwork is not scattered across email, phones and folders.

When a new driver starts

You tell us a driver is being onboarded. We send/maintain the document checklist, collect the records you provide, organize the file, flag missing items and track follow-up items.

Ongoing file maintenance

We maintain the file structure, update documents as replacements arrive, track recurring administrative items and keep an outstanding-items list.

Existing-file cleanup

If your current files are messy, we inventory what exists, organize it, identify missing or expired administrative items and give you a written gap list.

What you still control

You make hiring and safety decisions and provide required information, approvals, authorizations and documents. FleetDesk is administrative support, not legal or safety certification.

Add this to your back office

You can buy this as part of a bundle or include it in a custom mix of FleetDesk services.
