FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I choose only certain services?

Yes. FleetDesk can handle one back-office function, several individual services, or a bundled monthly package. We scope the work around what your operation actually needs.

Does FleetDesk replace my attorney, CPA, safety consultant, or dispatcher?

No. FleetDesk provides administrative and back-office support. We do not provide legal representation, tax preparation, CPA/attestation services, dispatching, formal safety certification, or guarantees of regulatory outcomes.

Is FleetDesk a dispatch service?

No. FleetDesk is focused on back-office administration: records, deadlines, billing paperwork, filing support, document organization, and basic bookkeeping support. Dispatch can be added later only if specifically offered.

Can you handle FMCSA Clearinghouse tasks?

We can assist with authorized administrative workflows after the carrier completes the required account setup, designations, consent steps, and permissions. The carrier remains responsible for its regulatory obligations.

Do you file IFTA and IRP for us?

We can organize mileage/fuel data, prepare workpapers, track deadlines, and support filings/renewals in the applicable jurisdiction. Exact filing authority and portal access depend on the carrier's base jurisdiction and authorization.

Are you a law firm, CPA firm, or tax preparer?

No. FleetDesk provides administrative and bookkeeping support. We do not provide legal advice, tax preparation, CPA attest services, or guarantee regulatory compliance.

How do you work with our documents?

At onboarding we agree on the tools and access method. FleetDesk can work within the carrier's approved portals, email workflow, or agreed cloud document system. Sensitive records should not be sent through unsecured public forms.

Can you clean up files that are already a mess?

Yes. Existing-file cleanup is a separate setup project. We inventory the current records, organize them, identify missing or expired administrative items, and create an outstanding-items report before monthly service begins.

What size fleet is this for?

The service is designed primarily for owner-operators and small fleets that have recurring office work but do not yet need or want a full-time administrative employee.

Regulatory context: FMCSA states that motor carriers must maintain a driver qualification file for each employed driver, employers of CDL drivers must conduct Clearinghouse queries at least annually, and carriers generally must retain ELD RODS/supporting documents for six months. IFTA is administered through member jurisdictions for motor-fuel use tax reporting. FleetDesk uses these requirements as workflow context but does not replace the carrier's legal responsibility. Official references: FMCSA DQ Files · FMCSA Clearinghouse Queries · FMCSA ELD Retention · IFTA, Inc.
