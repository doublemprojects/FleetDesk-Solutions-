FAQ

Clear boundaries before you buy.

Do I have to buy a bundle?

No. Every core service can be purchased separately. The bundles are simply discounted combinations for carriers that want several functions handled together.

Does FleetDesk replace my safety manager, attorney, CPA, tax preparer, dispatcher, or mechanic?

No. FleetDesk is an administrative/back-office service. We organize, track, prepare, follow up, and report. Decisions or work that legally belongs to the carrier, qualified professional, testing provider, medical provider, mechanic, or government agency stays with them.

Why are some prices “starting at”?

The workload is driven by drivers, trucks, loads, transactions, and the condition of existing records. We show useful starting prices and an estimated range before asking you to request a final quote.

Can FleetDesk work with my existing ELD, factoring company, accountant, or payroll provider?

Usually yes, provided the system allows appropriate access and the client authorizes it. We confirm systems and workflow during onboarding.

What happens if my fleet grows?

Your service scope and pricing are adjusted when driver, truck, load, or transaction volume moves beyond the agreed limits. You will know the new price before the expanded scope begins.
