How the scope works

The monthly price covers the recurring workload agreed during onboarding. Fleet size, driver count, load/invoice volume and unusually heavy cleanup can change the scope. Before service starts, FleetDesk gives you a written list of what is included so there is no mystery about what the monthly fee covers.


Third-party costs are separate: government fees, testing, insurance, software subscriptions, taxes and other outside charges are not included in FleetDesk’s monthly fee.
