What this plan is for

This is the lowest-friction way to stop running the compliance/admin calendar from memory without buying services you do not need.

Included services

  • Driver Files & Onboarding — ongoing file administration for up to 2 drivers.
  • Expiration & Renewal Tracking — agreed driver/vehicle/registration/insurance dates.
  • Vehicle Compliance Records — organized records for up to 2 trucks.
  • Monthly Owner Dashboard — due soon, missing, unresolved, and owner-action items.

Scope & limits

  • Up to 2 active drivers and 2 trucks.
  • Does not include IFTA filing admin, billing/A/R, bookkeeping, or driver settlement prep.
  • Government, testing, software, MVR, agency, and other third-party charges are separate.
  • One-time onboarding starts at $199; heavily disorganized/catch-up records may require a larger setup quote.
These limits are intentional. They keep the monthly price predictable for you and prevent the service from quietly turning into unlimited labor.
