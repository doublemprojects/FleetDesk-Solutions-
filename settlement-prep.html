Driver Settlement Preparation

Turn completed-load or mileage data into organized driver settlement worksheets for owner approval.

What problem this solves

Percentage pay, mileage pay, reimbursements, advances, and deductions can turn payroll preparation into repetitive manual work.

Exactly what FleetDesk handles

  • Collect completed-load/mileage inputs provided by the carrier.
  • Apply the carrier's written pay rules to prepare settlement calculations.
  • List reimbursements, advances, and authorized deductions supplied by the carrier.
  • Prepare a settlement statement/worksheet for review.
  • Maintain settlement-support documents in the driver's/pay-period folder.

FleetDesk handles

Routine administrative collection, organization, tracking, preparation, follow-up, and reporting within the agreed scope.

The carrier still handles

  • Defines lawful pay rules and approves every settlement.
  • Runs payroll/payment through its payroll provider, bank, or other payment system unless separately agreed.
  • Handles tax withholding, wage-law, classification, and legal questions with qualified professionals.

How the service works

1. CollectFleetDesk receives completed-load/mileage and adjustment data.
2. CalculateWe apply the carrier's documented compensation formula.
3. ReviewExceptions and missing information go back to the owner.
4. ApproveOwner approves the settlement.
5. ArchiveFinal worksheet and supporting records are stored.

Example

Five drivers finish the pay period. FleetDesk turns the completed-load data into five settlement worksheets so the owner reviews the numbers instead of building them from scratch.

Common questions

Do you run payroll?

The core service prepares settlement data. Payroll processing can remain with the carrier or its payroll provider.

Can you decide deductions?

No. FleetDesk applies only the written rules and authorized inputs provided by the client.

Can this be bundled with billing?

Yes; those two services often use the same completed-load records.
