IFTA & IRP Administration

Organize mileage, fuel, fleet, and renewal data so quarterly IFTA and IRP work does not become a last-minute scramble.

What problem this solves

IFTA and IRP both depend on accurate underlying fleet/mileage records. The administrative burden is gathering, reconciling, and submitting the right data on time—not simply clicking a filing button.

Exactly what FleetDesk handles

  • Collect mileage and fuel data supplied through the carrier's ELD, trip records, fuel reports, or other approved sources.
  • Identify obvious missing periods, duplicate entries, or unsupported totals for the carrier to resolve.
  • Prepare an organized IFTA filing worksheet and, when authorized, assist with online submission.
  • Track quarterly IFTA deadlines.
  • For IRP, collect vehicle/fleet/mileage information, prepare the renewal package, and track the renewal process.
  • Archive filed returns, confirmations, and renewed documents.

FleetDesk handles

Routine administrative collection, organization, tracking, preparation, follow-up, and reporting within the agreed scope.

The carrier still handles

  • Provides complete and accurate mileage/fuel/fleet records.
  • Reviews and approves filings, fees, and declarations.
  • Remains responsible for tax positions and regulatory accuracy; tax/legal questions go to the appropriate professional or agency.

How the service works

1. CollectCarrier sends mileage, fuel, and fleet data.
2. ReconcileFleetDesk checks the package for administrative gaps or inconsistencies.
3. PrepareWe organize the filing/renewal information for review.
4. ApproveCarrier reviews and approves before submission/payment.
5. ArchiveConfirmation and supporting records are stored with the period.

Example

Quarter-end arrives. Instead of the owner hunting through fuel receipts and state mileage at the deadline, FleetDesk has been organizing the inputs and produces the filing package for approval.

Common questions

Is IFTA included every month?

It is normally priced quarterly. The custom-plan calculator shows periodic fees separately from monthly services.

Do you provide tax advice?

No. FleetDesk provides administrative and bookkeeping support, not tax opinions or tax representation.

Can you handle IRP renewals?

Yes, as an administrative service subject to the carrier providing accurate data and authorization.
